D16 · Header background

The pinned header on a problem page. Each option sets it apart from the page background with a light kitchen touch; all colours are from the existing palette.

A · Order ticket

Card cream, like the problem card, with a small scalloped bottom edge echoing its ticket top. The quietest: lighter than the page, same feel as the cards.

B · Kitchen tiles

Light teal with faint tile grout lines and a teal line underneath. Reads as a tiled kitchen wall; ties the header to the "over" teal.

C · Shop awning

Soft terracotta with a terracotta and cream striped band below, like a restaurant awning. The most playful; ties to the "count" terracotta.

D · Counter top

A dark warm counter colour with a terracotta edge, in both themes. Strongest contrast, frames the page like a kitchen pass; the logo uses its brighter teal and terracotta to stay readable.
