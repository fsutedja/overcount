D9 · "New numbers" and "Try a variation"

Each problem has many checked number sets. These are three places to offer a new set (and, after solving, a harder variation of the same problem). The answer panel is shown after a correct answer.

A · "↻ New numbers" next to the title

A small link at the right of the problem title, always there: new numbers for the same problem whenever you like (before or after solving). No extra buttons after solving.

Problem

B · Buttons after solving

Nothing extra while you work. Once you get it right, two buttons appear under "✓ Correct": try the same problem with new numbers, or move on to a harder variation.

Problem

C · Both

The quiet "↻ New numbers" link by the title at any time (e.g. if the numbers feel awkward), plus the two buttons after solving as the clear next step.
