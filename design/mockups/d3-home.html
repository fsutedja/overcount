D3 · Home: roadmap, daily problem, activity

Same content in three layouts: the 8 stops of the learning path (counting stops list their methods with solved / total), today's problem, and a small activity heatmap. Existing colours and fonts only; stat labels in Pixelify Sans, numbers in Atkinson.

A · One path, top to bottom

The stops sit on one dotted line in learning order. Finished stops are filled teal, the one you're on is outlined and opens to show its methods; later stops are faded but still clickable. Today's problem and activity sit above the path. Works the same on phones.

B · Branching map (NeetCode-style)

Stops are laid out as a small map with connector lines, so you see which topics build on which (e.g. both Stars and bars and Complement follow Combinations). Each stop shows a progress bar; clicking one would open its methods. Today's problem and activity sit in a side column. On phones the map becomes a list.

C · Dashboard: today on the left, the whole path as a numbered list

A "today" column (problem, streak, heatmap) and a compact numbered list of all 8 stops with every method visible as a link and a coloured dot (green = some solved). Everything is one click away; the least "graph-like" of the three.
