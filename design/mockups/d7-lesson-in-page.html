D7 · Method lesson inside the page

Three ways to show the lesson without covering the answer box. In each, the answer box keeps its cursor and you can type while the lesson is open.

A · Pill opens the lesson right under it (like the Example toggle)

Clicking the pill expands the lesson inside the problem card, just below the statement; clicking again folds it. The answer pane on the right never moves. On phones the lesson sits between the problem and the answer box.
