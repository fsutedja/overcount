D14 · Linking the blanks to the diagram

Problem: choose 3 of 8 juniors and 2 of 6 seniors. The learner has clicked into the top blank of the "juniors" group (the number of juniors to choose from). Each option shows what lights up.

A · The Example diagram lights up

While a blank has focus, the matching pieces in the Example diagram on the left get a terracotta ring (here: all 8 juniors, "the juniors to choose from"); the rest fade. Uses the diagram you already see; on phones it's further away, above the answer box.

B · A tiny diagram under each group

Each part of the formula gets its own small row of pieces under its label (8 squares under "juniors", 6 under "seniors"); the ones the focused blank is about are highlighted. Everything is right next to the blanks, at the cost of a little more height.

C · A small hint card beside the focused blank

A small card appears next to the focused blank with a one-line hint in words and a mini picture ("How many juniors are there to choose from?"). No highlight elsewhere. The most explicit, but it's a hint that partly does the thinking.
