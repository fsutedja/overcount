D10 · Try dots for colour-blind readers, and Practice filters

Part 1: three ways to draw right / wrong tries. Each is shown as you'd see it and as someone with deuteranopia (the most common colour blindness) would see it. Part 2: three layouts for filtering problems by kind, difficulty and context.

Part 1 · Try dots

Now · filled green and filled berry

What the site does today. In the right-hand box the two colours become nearly the same, so right and wrong can't be told apart.

as most people see it

deuteranopia (simulated)

A · Wrong tries as rings

Right stays a filled green dot; wrong becomes a berry ring (hollow). Filled vs hollow works without colour, and it stays as compact as today.

as most people see it

deuteranopia (simulated)

B · Wrong tries as small ×

Right stays a filled green dot; wrong becomes a small berry ×. The most explicit: "×" reads as wrong even in print or black and white. A little busier in long rows.

as most people see it

Part 2 · Practice filters

Shown on a method's Practice tab (here: Combinations). Contexts are dice, cards, balls and urns, committees and seating.

A · A row of chips per filter

Kind, difficulty and context each get their own labelled row of chips. Everything visible at once, one tap to change; uses the most space.

Kind and difficulty as chips in one line, separated by thin dividers (like the problem card tabs); context as a single dropdown at the end. A middle ground.
