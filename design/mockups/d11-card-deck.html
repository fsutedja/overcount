D11 · Card-deck grid

For card problems, shown in the Example tab above the ✅/❌ lines. Problem: a 3-card hand counts if it's all hearts or all face cards. The ✅ hand here is K♣ Q♠ J♦ (all face cards). Rows are suits, columns are ranks; the hand's cards have a thick ink outline.

A · Shade both groups, stripes where they overlap

Hearts shaded teal, face cards shaded terracotta, the 3 heart face cards striped (in both groups). Shows the inclusion–exclusion overlap at a glance; the stripes and the legend keep it readable without colour.

B · Just the hand, groups marked by brackets

No group shading: the hand's cards are filled teal, everything else plain. The groups are named by labels along the edge ("hearts" beside the ♥ row, "face cards" above J Q K). The calmest; the overlap is left for the learner to spot.

C · Two small decks, one per group

Side by side: "all hearts" picks from the left deck's highlighted cards, "all face cards" from the right. The 3 cards highlighted in both are the overlap. Teaches "count each group, then subtract the overlap" most directly; takes two smaller grids.
